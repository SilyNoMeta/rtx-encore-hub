RTX Encore 2.0.0 and Hub 2.0.0

Compatibility depends on the game, GPU, API and runtime. Games without native DLSS use estimated motion and process the HUD with the image. Maintainer tests include The Sims 4, Skyrim and Elden Ring on RTX 4090; they do not certify every game or GPU. X5/X6 and Neural Rendering remain experimental.

Games without DLSS

Mod 2.0.0 brings DLSS Super Resolution or DLAA, Intel XeSS, Neural Rendering and frame generation to games that ship none, from the image they display, in DirectX 11, DirectX 12 and Vulkan. Tested in PEAK, The Sims 4, Skyrim Special Edition and Football Life 2027 on an RTX 4090.

Frame generation where there was none

DLSS Frame Generation X2 to X6 in DirectX 11, DirectX 12 and Vulkan, Intel XeFG up to X6 in DirectX 12, with NVIDIA Tensor Cores when available, in Vulkan (HDR10 included) and in DirectX 11. Change the multiplier while playing; the HUD is interpolated with the image.

Motion estimated by RTX Encore

The mod’s own motion estimation runs on the graphics card, and finds the game’s depth buffer by itself. vort_Motion’s shader method and NVIDIA Optical Flow are the other choices.

Choose your setup

At first installation in a game without native integrations, choose None, Smooth Motion, DLSS-G or XeFG, then None, DLSS or XeSS. No automatic Smooth Motion. The Hub previews the required files before installing.

Tune image quality

Ultra (75%) joins native DLSS SR and XeSS render scales. Custom sharpening offers a live 0–100% slider. Games without DLSS start with native anti-aliasing; use a lower windowed input resolution to upscale to your screen.

Keep compatible runtimes

Missing engines are not offered. Older compatible runtimes still work, with an update warning. The Hub helps install the recommended files; added DLSS-G uses its qualified bundle.

Neural Rendering, before and after

Neural Rendering is NVIDIA's network that works on the image DLSS produces: one to four passes, a style for each, HDR included. RTX Encore runs it on RTX 20, 30 and 40, with its own optimizations for RTX 30. Experimental, off by default.

The Blood of Dawnwalker at 2560 × 1440, frame generation X4, NR 310.9.16 working at 1472 × 828. The overlay in the corner gives the cost: 181 FPS without NR, 132 with it.

RTX Encore Hub does the installing

Finding folders, matching DLL versions, keeping backups: the Hub does it, and shows what it will do before it does it.

  1. Choose your setup

    At first installation in a game without native integrations, choose None, Smooth Motion, DLSS-G or XeFG, then None, DLSS or XeSS. No automatic Smooth Motion. The Hub previews the required files before installing.

  2. Smooth out X3 / X4: Automatic

    With mod 2.0.0, the mod learns per game whether Streamline should pace X3 and X4. The Hub shows what it learned; Measure again starts over.

  3. Your language, in game too

    The Hub now speaks twelve languages (thanks to the reporter of issue 12), and Mod menu language sets the in-game menu’s, now in eleven languages: Same as Hub by default, never over a language picked in game on install or update.

  4. Find a game with Ctrl+F

    From any page of Hub 1.6.0, Ctrl+F selects the game search in the title bar.

  5. Restore game can also clean up

    An unticked box also deletes the mod’s settings and logs, and its private runtime folder when it holds only the Hub’s files. Thanks to the reporter of issue 8.

  6. Update favorites only

    Hub 1.5.3 can limit bulk updates to starred games while keeping the library’s overall update status.

  7. XeSS and XeFG Tensor Cores on RTX 30/40/50

    Independent, default-off experimental controls. Mod 1.5.3 admits NVIDIA 621.01 and 621.12; only the earlier RTX 4090 / 621.01 setup has in-game qualification.

  8. V-Sync and RR preset F in Hub

    Hub 1.5.3 exposes V-Sync Game / Off / On and RR preset F. V-Sync requires compatible DirectX games, excludes XeSS-FG and can add latency.

Look before you install

What the Hub says of a game is deduced from its files, your card and your driver. It is not a certification that a feature works in that game, and it measures no performance.

Get started

  1. Download RTX-Encore-Hub-Setup-<version>.exe from the releases and run it: it installs for your account only. Or take the portable .zip, which installs nothing.
  2. Open a game's page in the Library, read what is possible there, and press Install.
  3. Under Health, Launch and verify checks the first minute. When a new RTX Encore is out, Update all.
Windows x64. The installer is not code-signed yet: Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time, and each release gives the SHA-256 of its installer.
Anti-cheat. Installing a mod in a game an anti-cheat protects can block its startup or put your account at risk. The Hub says so and writes nothing there without your word on the risk.
