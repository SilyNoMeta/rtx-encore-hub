RTX Encore 1.5.2 · Hub 1.5.2

XeSS and XeFG.
Now on NVIDIA Tensor Cores.

RTX Encore includes two independent experimental Tensor options: XeSS reconstruction and XeFG frame generation. Toggle them live from Hub 1.5.2 or the in-game menu. Off by default; initial qualification is RTX 4090, NVIDIA 621.01, DirectX 12 and the Intel runtimes supplied by Hub.

Intel XeSS / XeFG, Pre-SR, foveated NR and Smooth Motion on RTX 20 remain experimental. Compatibility depends on the game, graphics API and runtimes. Report an issue.

RTX Encore 1.5.2 and Hub 1.5.2

The menu and native Smooth Motion preparation were checked in Men of War II on an RTX 4090/621.01 test build. This does not measure interpolation, image quality or latency. RTX 50 hardware, native Vulkan and final binaries have no corresponding in-game qualification. Backup, download and settings checks use local automated tests.

Neural Rendering, before and after

Neural Rendering is NVIDIA's network that works on the image DLSS produces: one to four passes, a style for each, HDR included. RTX Encore runs it on RTX 20, 30 and 40, with its own optimizations for RTX 30. Experimental, off by default.

The Blood of Dawnwalker at 2560 × 1440, frame generation X4, NR 310.9.16 working at 1472 × 828. The overlay in the corner gives the cost: 181 FPS without NR, 132 with it.

RTX Encore Hub does the installing

Finding folders, matching DLL versions, keeping backups: the Hub does it, and shows what it will do before it does it.

  1. Mod 1.5.2: live V-Sync control

    Choose Game, Off or On in the in-game menu for compatible DirectX games. Not available with Vulkan or XeSS-FG; Hub 1.5.2 does not yet expose the new control.

  2. RTX 20: Smooth Motion V2

    Always uses V2. Hub 1.5.2 hides the retired variant choice and makes Hub updates more responsive.

  3. Two switches, your choice

    Use either option or both. They remain experimental and off by default; Hub requires mod 1.5.0.

  4. Profiles and update recovery

    Community profiles preserve both options. Hub retries transient update network failures with bounded attempts and required signature checks.

Look before you install

What the Hub says of a game is deduced from its files, your card and your driver. It is not a certification that a feature works in that game, and it measures no performance.

Get started

  1. Download RTX-Encore-Hub-Setup-<version>.exe from the releases and run it: it installs for your account only. Or take the portable .zip, which installs nothing.
  2. Open a game's page in the Library, read what is possible there, and press Install.
  3. Under Health, Launch and verify checks the first minute. When a new RTX Encore is out, Update all.
Windows x64. The installer is not code-signed yet: Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time, and each release gives the SHA-256 of its installer.
Anti-cheat. Installing a mod in a game an anti-cheat protects can block its startup or put your account at risk. The Hub says so and writes nothing there without your word on the risk.
