RTX Encore 1.2.2 · Hub 1.2.2

Frame generation and Neural Rendering on your RTX 20, 30 and 40.

RTX Encore brings DLSS frame generation up to X6, Neural Rendering and faster DLSS to RTX 20, 30 and 40. Version 1.2 adds experimental Intel engines, live reconstruction choices, NR settings per pass and live sharpening. RTX Encore Hub installs the mod and its runtimes, keeps games up to date and restores their original files.

Intel XeSS / XeFG remain very experimental. Compatibility depends on the mod, game, graphics API and runtimes. Report problems with a reviewed diagnostic archive; neither installed files nor a requested multiplier prove successful rendering. Report an issue.

What is new in RTX Encore 1.2

Mod 1.2.2 and Hub 1.2.2 are published separately. Sharpening is available in the mod’s menu; its Hub controls are for a later release. The Hub inspects files and runtime status; it does not certify image quality or displayed frames.

Live sharpening after reconstruction

Mod 1.2.2 adds Off / Game / Custom for DLSS, DLAA, Ray Reconstruction and replacement XeSS. Off by default; Custom defaults to 30%. Use the mod’s menu now; Hub controls are coming in a later release.

Choose DLSS or Intel engines

Choose DLSS or experimental XeSS reconstruction, and DLSS-G or experimental XeFG frame generation with XeLL. Install compatible runtimes through the Hub; routes depend on game inputs and graphics API.

Change reconstruction while playing

Change DLSS SR/RR presets or supported reconstruction engines live. In Vulkan games, install compatible Intel runtimes before launch; changing frame-generation engines still requires a restart.

Neural Rendering, pass by pass

Up to four NR passes, each with its own intensity and style or inherited settings. Common HDR-input and exposure controls follow the selected reconstruction engine; they do not turn on display HDR.

Smooth Motion and compatibility fixes

Smooth Motion supports driver 621.01 on RTX 30. Mod 1.2.2 fixes startup in SurrounDead, the Vulkan menu case in Baldur’s Gate 3 with OptiScaler F5, and a DLSS-G compatibility case with OptiScaler F5.

Image settings with clear limits

Sharpening adds to later game processing; high strengths can create halos and amplify noise. Its Vulkan operation, real-game appearance and performance remain unvalidated. Intel engines remain experimental.

Neural Rendering, before and after

Neural Rendering is NVIDIA's network that works on the image DLSS produces: one to four passes, a style for each, HDR included. RTX Encore runs it on RTX 20, 30 and 40, with its own optimizations for RTX 30. Experimental, off by default.

The Blood of Dawnwalker at 2560 × 1440, frame generation X4, NR 310.9.16 working at 1472 × 828. The overlay in the corner gives the cost: 181 FPS without NR, 132 with it.

RTX Encore Hub does the installing

Finding folders, matching DLL versions, keeping backups: the Hub does it, and shows what it will do before it does it.

  1. Export Hub logs

    Export recent Hub activity and errors from Settings or a failed game page, even when game pages cannot open. Review the archive before sharing.

  2. Smaller backups, reliable restoration

    Original files are kept in verified ZIP backups. Older uncompressed backups remain restorable and convert after a successful install or update; use Hub 1.2.2 or newer to restore compressed backups.

  3. Remembered choices

    The game page remembers preset, multiplier and option selections. Back and Apply remain accessible while scrolling; changes still need confirmation.

  4. A faster library

    Cached games appear first while verification runs in the background. Favorites, card actions, search, tray mode and remembered executable choices keep games within reach.

Look before you install

What the Hub says of a game is deduced from its files, your card and your driver. It is not a certification that a feature works in that game, and it measures no performance.

Get started

  1. Download RTX-Encore-Hub-Setup-<version>.exe from the releases and run it: it installs for your account only. Or take the portable .zip, which installs nothing.
  2. Open a game's page in the Library, read what is possible there, and press Install.
  3. Under Health, Launch and verify checks the first minute. When a new RTX Encore is out, Update all.
Windows x64. The installer is not code-signed yet: Windows may show a SmartScreen warning the first time, and each release gives the SHA-256 of its installer.
Anti-cheat. Installing a mod in a game an anti-cheat protects can block its startup or put your account at risk. The Hub says so and writes nothing there without your word on the risk.
