03 / What this comparison tells you

Detail versus sharpness

Look at roof fibres, railings and leaf edges. Stronger local contrast can look clearer without reconstructing additional detail. Watch for bright or dark outlines around edges.

Spatial versus injected SR

NIS is Encore’s spatial path. DLSS and XeSS here are supplied through Encore, not a native integration by the game developer. These captures do not represent every implementation of those algorithms.

A still image has limits

These are separate captures of an animated menu, not identical input frames. Smoke, foliage and lighting may differ. Still images cannot establish flicker, ghosting, frame pacing or GPU cost.

Capture conditions & limitations

¹ “No FG” is recorded in each supplied filename. NR was reported disabled by the owner. Input resolutions and mode names follow the supplied labels; file dimensions were checked independently. The four extra NIS captures are labelled FXAA or SMAA, PreNIS (before scaling) or PostNIS (after scaling), at 1080p with no FG. AA quality presets and strength were not supplied. Per-capture sharpness settings, runtime versions and capture timing were not recorded, so this is a visual exploration, not a controlled ranking.

“None” at 720p or 1080p is a low-resolution baseline, not native 1440p. The separate 1440p None image is the owner-labelled native reference. Replacement LS1 captures are 2560 × 1440. The corrected 1080p LS1 capture has no visible window borders. All original image pixels are retained.
