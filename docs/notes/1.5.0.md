# RTX Encore Hub 1.5.0

- **XeSS Tensor Cores and XeFG Tensor Cores:** two independent live controls for the new NVIDIA Tensor options in mod 1.5.0. Both remain experimental and off by default. Initial qualification: RTX 4090, NVIDIA 621.01, DirectX 12 and the compatible Intel runtimes supplied by Hub.
- **Live controls:** XeSS switches at its next execution; XeFG switches as recordings renew, with qualified fixed X2–X6 within initialized capacity. Installing the mod or changing the FG engine still requires restarting the game.
- **Community profiles:** preserve both Tensor choices independently; the online validator accepts the new fields. Their values do not establish hardware compatibility.
- **Hub updates:** retries transient network failures when checking or downloading an update, with bounded attempts. Signature checks remain required and installation is not retried.

The controls require **mod 1.5.0 or later**, so the previously published 1.4.7 does not expose unsupported switches. Unsupported Tensor preparation retains standard Intel execution; a Tensor execution fault requires restarting. No general XeFG FPS or latency improvement is established. Final Hub binaries have no separate in-game retest. [Mod 1.5.0](https://github.com/SilyNoMeta/rtx-encore/releases/tag/v1.5.0).
