# RTX Encore Hub 1.2.4

- **One copy of identical bytes.** Originals and history snapshots share compressed, verified backups across games. Different DLL contents remain distinct even when their version labels match. Unreferenced shared copies are collected safely.
- **Automatic migration from 1.0.x, 1.1.x and 1.2.x.** The first startup imports old originals and operation history, preserves settings and rollback, and removes only identified, unchanged old copies after the new references are committed. Migration resumes after interruption; locked or unavailable sources remain retryable. Unknown and altered files are retained.
- **Visible progress and deferred cleanup.** Stop between games and resume next startup. A separate administrator retry is available for protected game folders; startup never asks for elevation automatically.
- **RTX 20 Smooth Motion readiness.** With a compatible RTX Encore mod 1.3.0 or newer, driver 621.01 and an identified DirectX 12 game, the Hub can offer automatic RTX 20 GPU selection for Smooth Motion. Older mods and unconfirmed graphics APIs remain gated. This is not real-game validation.

Keep the complete Hub data folder. Migrated states require Hub 1.2.4 or newer for restoration. The runtime download store and eight-operation history limit are unchanged. Tests use fake games and pinned historical source engines; no real-game or performance validation is claimed.
