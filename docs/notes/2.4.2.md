## 2.4.2

Matches RTX Encore 2.4.2. The Hub numbers go from 2.4.0 to 2.4.2 to follow the mod.

### Korean

The Hub's interface is now available in Korean, its thirteenth language (thanks to the reporter of issue 17). **Mod menu language** also offers Korean for the in-game menu of RTX Encore 2.4.2; an older mod shows its menu in English. The Neural Rendering looks of RTX Encore 2.4.2 are named in Korean too, and this Hub is the one that offers them.

### Backups the Hub does not recognize no longer block anything

A game folder could hold an `rtx-encore-backups` folder or an `rtx-encore-backup-owner.txt` file that the current Hub did not write: after reinstalling the Hub with a new data folder, with the portable Hub beside the installed one, or with files copied from another PC. The Hub then refused every install, update and restore in that game (issue 15). It now only warns on the game's page. Those files are left as they are and never deleted; you can remove them yourself once you no longer need them.

Every 2.4.0 feature is unchanged: mod version selection and rollback, per-pass NR placement, custom DLSS scales down to 33% and Streamline SR dependency repair.

Neural Rendering remains experimental and off by default. Compatibility depends on the game, API, GPU, driver and runtime. No universal FPS or image-quality gain is promised.
