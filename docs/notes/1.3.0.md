# RTX Encore Hub 1.3.0

- **Foveated NR settings for RTX Encore 1.3.0.** A game's settings now offer the mod's experimental foveated Neural Rendering, off by default: turn on Foveated NR, then pick a preset (light, balanced, strong) or custom with its four values (width and height of the full-strength centre, transition length, detail kept at the edges). The NR effect is softer near the edges. The mod measured +27% average FPS with Strong in Cyberpunk 2077 on an RTX 3070 Ti Laptop. These settings can travel in community profiles.
- **Smooth Motion V1 or V2 on RTX 20.** With a detected RTX 20 card and RTX Encore 1.3.0, choose V1 (test 1, default) or V2 (test 2), then restart the game. The choice is hidden on other cards and never turns Smooth Motion on by itself. Driver 621.01 and DirectX 12 are still required.
- **OpenNR and the Cyber Engine Tweaks panel are gone.** RTX Encore 1.3.0 removed them, so the Hub no longer shows their settings; NVIDIA Neural Rendering stays. Settings left by the old panel remain readable until the mod imports them, and new settings are written beside the mod.
- **Storage usage in Settings.** See the space taken by the application, shared and local backups, downloaded runtimes, RTX Encore archives, game covers, logs and other Hub data, plus the older backups still kept in game folders. A partial scan says so, and its total is then a minimum.
- **Backup maintenance no longer repeats the same warning.** When a game folder holds a backup folder the Hub did not create, or the same game was recorded earlier under another identity, the Hub now finishes moving its own data and leaves that folder untouched. Remaining warnings name the game, and unprocessed files are kept.

Foveated NR and the RTX 20 V1/V2 choice require RTX Encore 1.3.0 or newer; older mods keep their current settings. Keep the complete Hub data folder: migrated states still require Hub 1.2.4 or newer for restoration. The Hub checks files and settings; it does not certify image quality or in-game performance. Tests use fake games; no real-game validation is claimed.
