# RTX Encore Hub 1.2.3

- **Reinstall cleanly, keep your settings.** A new action on a Hub-managed game's page previews the reinstall, recreates the mod and its managed runtimes, and preserves your settings exactly, including comments. Health keeps a way back to the previous installation. Missing exact runtime sources or unknown files refuse the operation before game files are written. Thanks to [@wer2357666](https://github.com/wer2357666) for the suggestion and reports ([#4](https://github.com/SilyNoMeta/rtx-encore-hub/issues/4)). This is a repair option, not a confirmed fix for the reported GTA V Enhanced crashes.
- **Sharpening controls and shared preferences.** Choose Off, Game or Custom sharpening for compatible RTX Encore versions, with a strength from 0 to 100% and Custom starting at 30%. The preference can be included in shared profiles. These controls now ship in the Hub; RTX Encore 1.2.2 provides the corresponding mod feature. High strengths can amplify noise and halos.

The Hub and RTX Encore mod have separate versions. This release does not change the mod. Automated checks used fake game folders; they do not establish in-game compatibility, visual quality or performance. Intel XeSS and XeFG remain experimental.
